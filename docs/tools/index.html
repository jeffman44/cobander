Free tools for songwriters and bands

Quick tools for the jobs that come up every practice. They run in your browser — nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

  • Chord transposer and capo calculator

    Paste a chord chart or a progression and move it up or down by half steps. Lyrics stay where they are. Plus a capo calculator: which shapes to play for any key. Free, no sign-up.

  • What key is my song in?

    Type the chords of your song and find its key, with Roman numeral analysis (I–V–vi–IV) and any chords borrowed from outside the key. Free, works in your browser.

  • Tap tempo: find the BPM

    Tap along to find a song's BPM, then get the delay times in milliseconds for quarter, eighth, dotted eighth and triplet notes. Free, works on phone and desktop.

  • Song structure builder

    Lay out your song's sections — intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, outro — with bar counts, and see when each part starts and how long the song runs at your tempo. Free.
