Getting an accurate count

Tap on the kick and snare, not the hi-hat — the main beat is what tempo counts. The counter averages your last eight taps, and stopping for two seconds starts a fresh count. If the number looks like half or double what you feel, the half-time and double-time values are right under it.

Delay times

A delay set to the song's tempo sits in the groove instead of smearing it. A quarter note at 120 BPM is 500 ms; a dotted eighth (three quarters of a beat) gives the rhythmic echo guitarists love — 375 ms at 120 BPM.

Questions

How is BPM calculated?

From the average time between your taps: 60,000 divided by the milliseconds per beat.

What is a dotted eighth delay?

Three-quarters of a beat. At 120 BPM a beat is 500 ms, so a dotted eighth is 375 ms.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Tap the button with your thumb.
