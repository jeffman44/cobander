Free & Send noods

Groupie, the free version, is the whole app. Sending noods buys a key that lifts the one-song limit for everyone in the band: $25 for the band, once.

What's free

  • Everything: Write, Record, Mix, Practice, the Band page, sync, updates. Windows and Mac.
  • The only limit is one song per band folder. Folders that already hold more songs keep them all.
  • The Noodle pad is your own scratch song and doesn't count.

What a key does

  • A Send noods key lifts the limit for the whole band (up to 8 people). One key, not one per person or per computer.
  • It lives in the shared folder. Whoever pastes it unlocks everyone.
  • It checks itself on your computer, never phones home, and never expires. It keeps working if Cobander stops being developed.
  • It carries your band's name (and bandmates' names, if you gave them). With a key in the folder, Cobander calls the band by that name.
  • It can be switched off if it's posted publicly or resold — you'd get a fresh one. A switched-off key puts that folder back on free; no songs are lost. Sharing it with your own band is the point.

What it costs

  • Free: Groupie. Everything, one song per band folder.
  • $25: Roadie. One key for the whole band.
  • $60: VIP. The same key, plus your band's name on the VIP wall, on cobander.com and inside the app under ⚙. Legends never die.
  • Get it from the Send noods button under ⚙, on the Band page, or on cobander.com. Give your band's name (and your bandmates' names if you like). The key arrives by email, usually within a minute.

Enter the key

  1. Copy the whole key from the email. It looks like cob1.xxxx.xxxx. Extra spaces and line breaks from the email are fine.
  2. In Cobander, click ⚙ → Enter a key, paste it, and click Unlock the band.
  3. Or wait for the second song: when you make it, Cobander asks. Paste the key right there.
  • ⚙ and the Band page then show who the key is licensed to instead of "Groupie (free): one song per band folder".
  • Bandmates don't need to do anything. The key is in the folder they already share.

Lost the key

  • Reply to the email it came in, or write to noods@cobander.com. It gets re-sent.
  • Moved the band to a new folder? Paste the key again in the new one.

Refunds

  • If the key doesn't work and it can't be fixed within a few days of you saying so, you get your money back in full.
  • Change-of-mind refunds within 14 days are fine as long as the key hasn't been used. Just ask.
  • The small print is at cobander.com/terms.

Extra sauce

  • Already have it and want to say thanks? Extra sauce is a tip, any size, on Venmo (@jdeuel44) or Ko-fi. The links are under ⚙ and on the Band page.

The VIP wall

  • Bands who sent noods at the VIP level are listed under ⚙ → VIPs and on cobander.com.
  • Your name goes up once you've confirmed how you'd like it to read. Names that are offensive or aren't yours can be declined.

Privacy, in short

  • The app sends nothing anywhere. No accounts, no telemetry, no crash reports.
  • It makes two read-only network requests: checking for updates, fetching the VIP list, and fetching the list of switched-off keys.
  • Speech-to-text, chord detection, key detection and pitch check all run on your computer.
  • The source is open (MIT). The key check is one function anyone can read. It's a tip jar with a nudge, not copy protection.
