Song structure builder

Start from a common song form, then change sections, bar counts and tempo. You see where each part starts and how long the whole song runs — handy for setlists and for telling the band what comes next.

    Bars, beats and song length

    A bar of 4/4 is four beats. At 120 BPM a beat is half a second, so a bar is two seconds and an 8-bar verse is 16 seconds. Most sections are 4, 8 or 16 bars long; the 12-bar blues is the famous exception.

    See the song structure templates guide for what each section does and when to break the rules.

    Questions

    How is the song length worked out?

    Bars × beats per bar × 60 ÷ BPM, added up across the sections.

    Can I use odd time signatures?

    Yes. Set beats per bar to 3 for 3/4, 6 for 6/8 counted in eighths, and so on.

    Does it save my song?

    No — copy the text when you are done. In the Cobander app, the structure is saved in the song with the recordings.
