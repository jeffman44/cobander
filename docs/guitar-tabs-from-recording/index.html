Play the riff. Get the tab.

Record the guitar or bass part and Cobander drafts the chord chart and the tab straight from the audio — bar by bar, on your computer, no upload. Your bandmates learn the part from the tab instead of from a phone video.

Chords from the audio

Cobander listens to the takes and writes the chord chart for the song, with the bass line naming the roots. It lands on the song's chord lane, so the chart, the practice view and the lyric sheet all line up.

Tabs you can play along with

Guitar and bass takes become tab with real chord shapes, not just chord names. Lead and rhythm guitar each get their own scrolling play-along lane, so each player can practice the part that is actually theirs.

Fix it when it guesses wrong

Transcription is a draft. The tab editor lets you change any note or fret, and the fix is saved to the song for the whole band.

Hear the key, then move it

Cobander also hears what key the song is in from the recordings. Need it a step down for the singer? Transpose the whole song — the recordings, the MIDI and the chords move together.

Questions

Does it work on bass too?

Yes. Bass takes get tab and a play-along lane the same way guitar does.

Does my audio get uploaded to transcribe it?

No. Chord and tab detection run on your own computer.

How accurate is it?

It is a strong first draft for clean, recorded parts. Busy distorted parts need more fixing — that is what the tab editor is for.
