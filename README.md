# Cobander — free songwriting & recording software for bands

Cobander keeps your whole band on the same song. Everyone writes, records, mixes and practices from their own computer, and the song syncs through the Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox folder your band already shares — no Cobander server, no accounts, no subscription. Every take, lyric, chord and practice minute earns XP, so the band levels up as it works.

**Download:** [Windows](https://takeout.cobander.com/download/win) · [Mac, Apple silicon](https://takeout.cobander.com/download/mac-arm64) · [Mac, Intel](https://takeout.cobander.com/download/mac-x64) — or the [latest release](https://github.com/jeffman44/cobander/releases/latest).

Free: the whole app, one song per band folder. A one-time license per band (up to 8 people) unlocks unlimited songs.

## What it does
- **Write together:** sections, chords and lyrics on one timeline; notes pinned to bars; @mentions; a stage for every part from idea to approved.
- **Record:** multitrack takes, punch-ins, comping, click and count-in, tuner, drum generator, amp sims.
- **Hear what you played:** lyrics typed out from a vocal take, chords drafted from guitar, keys and bass takes, the song's key heard from the recordings, a sung line turned into melody notes.
- **Mix:** a real console with EQ, compression, sends, Auto mix and Auto master, stems.
- **Practice:** lyrics and chords that follow the song, loops, slow-down without pitch change, play along with your own part taken out.
- **Level up:** XP, 100 levels, a band level, achievements and countdowns to your next show.
